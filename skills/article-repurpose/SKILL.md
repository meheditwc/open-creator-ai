# Article Repurpose

A video lives on YouTube; the same answer written down lives on search for years.

## When to use

- A video is published and performing.
- The user wants a blog post, newsletter issue or site article from it.

## The run order

1. **Article Writer** — give it the video's topic. You get a headline, standfirst,
   sections that mirror the video's beats, a video embed placeholder and a
   closing CTA, as Markdown plus a self-contained HTML file.
2. Edit the HTML or Markdown in your own voice, then paste the video URL into
   the embed slot.

## What "done" looks like

- A headline that works as a search result, not a slogan.
- Sections a reader can skim and still get the answer.
- One internal link and one CTA — not five.

## Rules

- Do not paste the script in; rewrite for readers who cannot see the b-roll.
- Keep the article independent of the video — the reader who never presses play
  should still get the answer.
